Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VeriCargo student project ("VeriCargo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when an authorized tester uses the VeriCargo Chrome extension and its supporting cloud service.

1. VeriCargo's purpose

VeriCargo is an AI-assisted logistics document-verification tool. It classifies shipping-related Gmail messages, identifies Shipping Instructions (SI) and Draft Bills of Lading (Draft BL), extracts and normalizes shipment information, compares document fields, flags discrepancies, and supports human-review and Gmail-draft workflows.

2. Information we process

When a user affirmatively connects a Google account, VeriCargo may process:

  • Google account information: the authorized account's email address and Gmail profile information needed to establish the connection.
  • Gmail message information: message and thread identifiers, sender name and address, subject, message body or snippet, received time, Gmail labels, and related message metadata.
  • Attachment information: filenames, file types, file sizes, attachment identifiers, and attachment contents when needed for an attachment preview, document identification, extraction, OCR, or comparison.
  • Logistics document information: extracted and normalized fields such as shipper, consignee, notify party, ports, container count, gross weight, evidence snippets, comparison outcomes, and confidence indicators.
  • Workflow information: classifications, label selections, review status, reviewed draft content, generated-file details, response-draft details, and timestamps for user-requested workflow actions.
  • Authentication and connection information: an encrypted Google OAuth refresh token on the VeriCargo backend, short-lived authorization state, and VeriCargo session tokens. The extension does not collect the user's Google password.
  • Basic operational information: service errors and technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to provide and support VeriCargo's disclosed logistics-document workflow, including to:

  • connect to Gmail with the user's authorization;
  • synchronize relevant Inbox messages and supported VeriCargo labels;
  • classify logistics emails and identify required documents;
  • extract, normalize, compare, and display shipping-document information;
  • show attachment previews requested by the user;
  • save human-review decisions and document-comparison status;
  • generate normalized files and create Gmail drafts requested by the user; and
  • maintain the security, reliability, and integrity of the service.

We do not sell user information, use it for personalized advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to VeriCargo's stated functionality.

4. Google API Limited Use

VeriCargo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Humans do not read users' Gmail content except when the user gives specific consent for support, when necessary to investigate security or abuse, when required by law, or after the information has been aggregated and anonymized for permitted internal operations.

5. Services that process information

VeriCargo uses the following service providers to deliver its functionality:

  • Google Gmail API to access authorized Gmail information, update supported labels, and create drafts requested by the user.
  • Google Cloud Run and Google Cloud Firestore to operate the VeriCargo backend and store connection, message, classification, extraction, and workflow records.
  • Google Gemini API to classify relevant email content and perform document understanding or OCR when required. Relevant message text and attachment content may be sent to Gemini for these purposes.
  • Chrome local storage to store a VeriCargo session token and temporary connection polling token on the user's device.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service from security threats, or as otherwise permitted by the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy. We do not transfer information to data brokers or advertising networks.

6. Storage, security, and retention

VeriCargo transmits information over HTTPS. Google OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted before backend storage, and the backend runs on Google Cloud services with platform security protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to this student test project.

Backend message and workflow records are retained while needed to provide and test VeriCargo. During Gmail synchronization, records are removed when their corresponding messages are no longer in the Gmail Inbox. Connection records and encrypted OAuth tokens may remain until they are deleted by the project team or upon an approved deletion request. Temporary VeriCargo sessions expire after 30 days unless ended earlier.

7. User choices and deletion

  • Users can disconnect the active VeriCargo browser session from the extension.
  • Users can revoke VeriCargo's Google access through their Google Account connections.
  • Users can request deletion of their VeriCargo backend records by emailing the address below from the Google account connected to VeriCargo.

We may need to retain limited information when required for security, legal compliance, or resolving abuse.

8. Children's privacy

VeriCargo is intended for logistics workflow testing and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VeriCargo changes. We will update the effective date on this page and provide any additional notice required by applicable policy or law. Materially different data practices will be disclosed before the new practices begin.
