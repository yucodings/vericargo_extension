Gmail logistics assistant

Review shipping emails and documents with confidence.

VeriCargo is an AI-assisted Chrome extension that helps logistics users classify Gmail messages, verify Shipping Instructions against Draft Bills of Lading, identify discrepancies, and complete human-review workflows.

  • Email triage
    Organizes incoming logistics messages into practical workflow categories.
  • SI/BL verification
    Extracts, normalizes, and compares important shipment fields.
  • Human review
    Surfaces uncertain, missing, ambiguous, and unreadable cases for review.
  • Draft assistance
    Creates reviewable normalized Draft BL files and Gmail response drafts.
VeriCargo provides AI-assisted results for human review. Users should verify shipping information against the source documents before confirming or sending it.
